# Production launch gates

This starter is not production-ready. Do not accept real funds or launch tokens until all relevant gates are complete:

- Authentication, MFA, secure recovery, RBAC and immutable admin audit logs.
- Age assurance, creator identity/consent verification, moderation, reporting and takedown controls.
- Compliant adult-content hosting and distribution review; keep adult experiences off native apps unless expressly permitted.
- Private media storage and server/CDN-side playback authorization.
- Solana Devnet payment verifier first; validate cluster, transaction finality, viewer, stream, mint, recipient, amount and replay uniqueness.
- Non-custodial wallet signing; no seed phrase/private key collection.
- Multisig treasury, limits, monitoring and emergency pause.
- Managed database, backups, secrets vault, WAF, rate limits, alerts and incident response.
- Legal review for token issuance, exchange routing, payments, consumer protection, privacy and applicable licensing.
- Independent smart-contract audit and application penetration test.
